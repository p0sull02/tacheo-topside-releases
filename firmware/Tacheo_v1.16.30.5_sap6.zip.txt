superseded 2026-11-10 by 1.16.30.4 (same battery fix + the new menus) - 1.16.30.3 was never tried on a SAP6:
Tacheo firmware 1.16.30.3 - SAP6 (Phil's SAP6 running Tacheo, the frozen 1.16.30 line) - 10 Nov 2026. On Sully's PC only, NOT on Google Drive.

WHAT AND WHY
Sully, 10 Nov 2026: "make sure the sap6 gets the battery update. It did seem to work on the tacheo."
TCH-260004 (Tacheo 1, colour) lost 6-10 mV an hour while fully off (System OFF) and held 0.6-0.9 mV an hour for 20 h in the
parked standby. Fully off was the drain, not the standby. Phil's own SAP6 firmware never went fully off either.

HOW (built on 1.16.30.2)
1. STAY IN STANDBY. Switched off, the SAP6 parks (as since 1.16.30.1: log flash asleep, buses and clocks off) and stays
   there - it no longer goes fully off 2 h later. A double-click wakes it straight to READY on the same station.
   Fully off only when the cell is nearly flat (under 3.45 V), to protect it; plugged in after that and unplugged, it
   goes back to standby, not fully off. The battery is still read every 30 s while off.
2. FPU INTERRUPT OFF AGAIN. 1.16.30.2 turned it on; it did nothing for the drain, and its handler could write to stale
   stack memory (the same handler crashed the Tacheo 1 on 1.16.98 / 1.17.0). The FPU flags and pending bit are still
   cleared at the park.
3. CRASH RECORD SURVIVES THE RESTART. It sat in the stack the board core paints at every start, so no crash or freeze
   ever kept its address. Now the top 64 bytes of the heap. CRASHTEST! over USB proves it.

TEST FOR SULLY (his SAP6)
Charge, unplug, switch off. Leave it 1-2 days. Double-click: READY at once (no cold start). Plug into Topside, send a report:
the trip log's R record should show the hours in standby and only a few mV lost.

SIMULATOR (SAP6 1.16.30 line, 145 scenarios, against 1.16.30.2 from the same sim)
- All 144 existing scenarios identical to 1.16.30.2 (the simulator keeps its old fully-off-at-2-h rule for them).
- New v116303_stay, 5/5: parked at 4.00 V; still parked, never fully off at 3 h and 11 h; a double-click is READY at once;
  nearly flat (3.40 V) = fully off after the 2 h.

BUILD: 478,504 B flash (59 %).


released 2026-11-09 (on Sully's PC only, never tried on a SAP6; superseded by 1.16.30.3 - its FPU interrupt is off again)
# 9 Nov 2026: the SAP6 (Phil's SAP6 running Tacheo). Sully: "go ahead with ... building and staging, updates for the 5xc, and sap6". 1.16.30.1 plus only the drain research's two sleep fixes (source: Breadboard\Sketches\Tacheo SAP6 (1.16.30 line)).
# On Sully's PC only until a SAP6 has tried it: make_release_zip.py HOLDs this zip and .txt, and 1.16.30.1 stays in firmware\ as the released one. When it's good: out of HOLD, 1.16.30.1 into firmware\older\, the manifest's sap6 line 1.16.30.2.
- FIX (standby drain): a floating-point flag left by almost any sum keeps the chip's FPU interrupt pending, and a pending interrupt stops the processor sleeping (nRF52840 errata 87). The colour Tacheo 1's standby showed exactly that: the processor awake 89.7 % of the time with the FPU interrupt pending. The FPU interrupt now runs and clears the flags where the interrupted code keeps them (Nordic's fix that holds under FreeRTOS); the flags and the FPU and QSPI pending bits are also cleared when the standby parks and before going fully off.
- Diagnosis report: SBPARK adds pend= (interrupts pending at the park), seen= (any noticed while parked) and fpu= (how often the FPU interrupt ran).
- Nothing else changes: menus, shots, calibration, CrossTalk, Bluetooth are 1.16.30's.
- Built 9 Nov 2026 (478,244 B, 58 % of flash; +368 B). In the SAP6 1.16.30 firmware simulator all 144 scenarios give the same results as 1.16.30.1 (the standby park and Drain test scenarios pass; SBPARK shows the new fields). The interrupt itself only runs on a unit. Not yet tried on a SAP6.
