  Messages from the Topside phone app (Survey and sketch > the chat panel):
  - The phone sends MSG=<text> / MSG+=<more> / MSGGO=<id> over Bluetooth (the console, Nordic UART). USB works too.
  - The message pops up over whatever page is showing, with a chime (two rising notes): the page dimmed round it (OLED: every
    other dot; colour: each colour a step darker, the battery pill as it is) and the message in a box in the middle,
    MESSAGE / phone in its title, up to 100 characters.
  - Any button puts it away; that press does nothing else. The page comes back exactly as it was.
  - It waits while the unit can't be interrupted: a shot being measured, a calibration hold, the tap-countdown, a cross-section
    sweep, standby, the start-up animation - and shows the moment that's over.
  - The unit tells the phone MSG SHOWN <id> when it's up and MSG READ <id> when a button put it away (the phone's ticks).
  - Settings > Messages: the last 10, newest first, with the time the phone set (TIME=) - pick one to see it in the same box.
    Kept in /msglog.bin over a restart.
  - MSG? answers MSG <IDLE|WAITING|SHOWING> <id> <shown> <read> <kept> (and tells the phone this firmware has messages).

How:
  msgCommand() in handleCommand; msgService() at the end of service() (every page's wait calls it); msgPopup() keeps the
  page's picture (the 128 x 64 buffer, the 1.5" buffer, and on the colour screen its 38 KB frame in /msgpage.bin on the
  2 MB chip - there is no RAM for a second frame), draws the dimmed page and the box, waits for a button (service() still
  runs; awake while it waits, away by itself after 10 min), and puts the picture back. MsgHoldGuard in measureShot() and
  capturePoint(). Settings > Messages is the last row before Back (SET_MSGS; the sim's SETTINGS_MSGS_ROW).
  sap5x: unfrozen (it was held at 1.16.30 since 21 Oct) - everything from 1.16.31 to 1.17.8 comes with it.

Simulator (Firmware sim, scenario v1178_msg - over READY, over the menu, held, MSG?, Settings > Messages):
  cave 231 scenarios, cavec 231, sap5xc 231, sap5x 231: the same results as 1.17.7 apart from v1178_msg (13/13 on each);
  cavec v11657_spam's long-standing "sent N transfers too early" note moved by one transfer (timing). ASan/UBSan clean
  (cavec: v1178_msg, settings_persist, v11688_settings). The colour page's keeping on the 2 MB chip can't run in the
  simulator (no chip there: it draws the page again instead) - CHECK IT ON THE UNIT.

Real builds: cave 638,112 B (78 %), RAM 64 %; cavec 790,028 B (97 %), RAM 87 %; sap5xc 774,812 B (95 %), RAM 86 %;
  sap5x 564,760 B (69 %). 0 unaligned literal loads.

Test it (your unit, before the fleet):
  1. Flash it. In the phone app (Android 1.7): a survey > 📡 > connect > 💬, send "Test message one" to the unit.
  2. On READY: the chime, the page dimmed, the box in the middle. Press SHOT: gone, READY as it was, no shot taken.
     The phone's bubble: ✓ sent, ✓✓ on screen, Read.
  3. Open the menu and send another: it pops up over the menu; MENU puts it away and you're still in the menu.
  4. Colour unit: the page after the box must be the colour page exactly as it was (this is the 2 MB chip path).
  5. Take a shot and send one while it measures: it waits, then shows when the result is up.
  6. Settings > Messages: both listed, newest first; pick one: the same box.
  7. Restart the unit: Settings > Messages still has them.

released 2026-11-09 (on Sully's PC only - not on Google Drive)
Tacheo firmware 1.16.98 (Tacheo 1 colour screen = cavec, Tacheo 1 OLED = cave, SAP5 with the colour screen = sap5xc)
- READY: the laser dot is always lit (Sully, 9 Nov 2026: "When on ready, always have the laser on. Whenever a user wants the laser off,
  he can take a shot and keep it on the data screen."). No minute's time-out on READY, no laser switch-off with it, and the dot is lit
  as soon as READY shows after switching on. It still goes out on the data screen (a shot's result), in the menus and in standby.
- Standby: Nordic's fix for the FPU interrupt (nRF52840 errata 87, DevZone 12433). Ordinary float maths sets FPSCR's flags, which pend
  the FPU interrupt (IRQ 38), and a pending interrupt stops the processor sleeping (TCH-260004 on 1.16.96: 89.7 % awake in standby,
  IRQ 38 pending). The FPU interrupt is now enabled at the lowest priority and its handler clears the FPSCR saved on the stack, which
  FreeRTOS restores - clearing it before each sleep alone (1.16.97) can be undone by a task switch.
- The QSPI interrupt (IRQ 41) is cleared when the logs' flash is closed and while parked (DevZone 55476).
- DIAG SLEEPIRQ: FPU interrupts handled, FPU / QSPI pendings cleared in standby, the interrupts seen pending in two standby looks in a
  row (stuck), all seen, and those pending now.
- cavec keeps 1.16.97's loggers (STBYLOG, OFFCRUMB, OFFPINS2, Device > Fully-off test) and quiet park. cave and sap5xc get the shared
  code since their last versions (cave 1.16.95, sap5xc 1.16.93: 1.16.95's laser kept powered for compass reads).
Test: READY left 2 minutes - the dot stays lit; a shot - the data screen, dot off; a press - READY, dot lit at once. Standby 1-2 days
unplugged, then a Topside report: STBYLOG awake % a few % (cavec), SLEEPIRQ stuck=00000000/00000000.


released 2026-11-04
# 4 Nov 2026: SAP5 with a XIAO and the colour screen (sap5xc). Everything in 1.16.92, plus (Sully: "fix all issues", after Paul's reports):
- FIXED: a calibration that "failed to save" (Paul's colour T1, TCH-A70CD4C0: a Normal calibration finished at 0.17 and was gone after the next restart; the same unit had lost its serial number on 28 Oct). The unit's internal storage had stopped taking writes, so the new calibration lived in memory only. Now a save that fails repairs the storage on the spot - wipes it and writes back everything in memory (settings with the new calibration, serial number, owner, Options, QC, screen settings, history, backsight pairs) - and saves again. Once a power-on.
- NEW: a storage check at every switch-on from cold: a small write read back. Storage that takes no writes is repaired before anything else is saved to it.
- What a repair loses: the saved calibration holds and calibration records from before (the calibration itself is kept). The 2 MB chip (logs, animations, fonts) isn't touched. The trip log gets an F record; Topside 1.6.55 shows it ("The internal storage repaired itself"). DIAG adds FSHEAL repairs=N.
- SERIAL= / SERIAL!= from Topside repairs the storage and tries again if its write fails.
- AFTER INSTALLING on a unit that showed "Axes are not set" or lost its calibration: Factory > Axes (level, laser to north; then laser straight up), then Normal > All sensors outdoors.
- Built 4 Nov 2026. Simulator: the full scenario list on the sap5xc build, the same results as 1.16.92. The repair itself runs only on the hardware (the simulator has no flash). Not tried on a unit yet.
- Install: plugged in and running, Topside offers it (Updates and Upgrades > Tacheo). Or copy Tacheo_v1.16.93_sap5xc.uf2 onto the XIAO's drive (reset button twice quickly).
