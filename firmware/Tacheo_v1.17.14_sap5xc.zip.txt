Tacheo firmware 1.17.14 - side leads from the phone, and an update mode that can always be finished
=======================================================================
For units coming from 1.17.11 this brings 1.17.12 - 1.17.14:
- Side leads named from the phone app: station series may hold - and . (A25-B -> A25-B1), and the phone can start a side lead from any station.
- A saved cross section tells the phone app.
- Update mode now shows the XIAO-SENSE drive, so an update that stops partway can be finished by copying the file onto it (Tacheo 1.6.112+ does this itself).

Tacheo firmware 1.17.14 (cave, cavec, sap5xc) - 23 Nov 2026

What and why
Sully, 23 Nov 17:01: "Can I currently control the stations on the phone app? Say I want to do a side lead, can I manually edit the next station designation to be A25-B1?"; 17:02: "Add that in, and have the Tacheo read the change and update as well."
- The unit's series names were letters and digits only, so the phone could not tell it about a side lead named A25-B: STNAT=A25-B1 was refused (STN BAD STATION) and the phone only ever sent the series' letters.
- There was no way to start a side lead from the phone; only the unit's own Stations > Side lead (series AA, AB ...).

How
- A series name may now hold - and . (not as its first character): A25-B gives stations A25-B1, A25-B2 ...; A25-B2.C works for a lead off the lead. Letters-and-digits names work as before. Up to 10 characters, as before.
- New command STNLEAD=<from>,<series> (USB and Bluetooth, the same handler as STN? / STNSET / STNAT): the series is added if new, the unit is at <from> and its next leg runs <from> to <series>1, exactly as Stations > Side lead does on the unit (READY shows "At A25>A25-B"). A lead already surveyed keeps the station it left. A lead name ending in a digit is refused (A25 + 1 would read A251). Replies OK STN ... or STN BAD LEAD.
- Back on the main line the phone sends STNAT=A25 as before.
- The phone app (cartotable-app 5374c18, live) sends these: Data > Change > "Start a side lead from this station", or tap a station in the plan > "Start a side lead here"; "Back to the main line" restores the main series and its next number. With firmware older than 1.17.14 the phone still names every shot and says once that the unit needs 1.17.14.
- Everything else as 1.17.13 (the UF2 update mode included). sap6 not rebuilt (its own sketch line).

Sim (cavec): new scenario v11714_stnlead 17/17 pass (STNLEAD, legs A25-A25-B1, backsight, A25-B1-A25-B2, STNAT back to A25 -> A26, STNAT=A25-B2 picks the lead up again, a lead off the lead with a dot, bad names refused, kept on flash, LOG lines). The full suite old (1.17.13) vs new: identical on all 243 shared scenarios (2198 checks; the same 54 failures in both - not new).
Size: cavec 796,676 of 811,008 bytes (98%), about 500 bytes more than 1.17.13.

Test for Sully (bench, phone app + a unit on 1.17.14, Leg track On)
1. Phone: open a survey, Data > Change > Start a side lead from this station (e.g. A25, series A25-B). The unit's READY should read "At A25>A25-B".
2. Shoot a leg: phone and unit both call it A25 -> A25-B1; the next one A25-B1 -> A25-B2.
3. Phone: Back to the main line (at A25). The unit should read "At A25"; the next leg A25 -> A26 on both.


Tacheo firmware 1.17.13 (cave, cavec, sap5xc, sap6) - 22 Nov 2026 - NOT ON THE FIRMWARE CHANNEL (placed in Topside on Sully's PC to try first)

What and why
Sully, 22 Nov 08:27: "You need the change the code so something like this doesn't happen in the future. It's happened 2x times now."
- Zach's colour T1 (TCH-260006): Topside 1.6.109's USB update to 1.17.10 on 19 Nov erased the old firmware (single bank: 796 KB doesn't fit twice), then stopped at about 88% with adafruit-nrfutil "Write timeout". No firmware to start. Its update mode was serial only (the 1200-baud touch -> enterSerialDfu, GPREGRET 0x4E): no XIAO-SENSE drive, and on battery that session ends only with the reset button or a flat battery (unplugging does nothing). Three more tries (21-22 Nov) got no answer from the stuck session, and the .uf2 had nowhere to go. The T1 colour's reset button can't be reached.
- Sully's own unit died the same way over Bluetooth on 20 Nov.

How
- Update mode now shows the drive. The Seeed core's 1200-baud touch handler (TinyUSB_Port_EnterDFU in Adafruit_TinyUSB_nrf.cpp - not weak, so the sketch can't override it) now calls enterUf2Dfu (GPREGRET 0x57, the bootloader's UF2 mode) instead of enterSerialDfu. UF2 mode has the same serial port, so adafruit-nrfutil still works, plus the XIAO-SENSE drive. An update that stops part-way leaves the drive there, and the .uf2 can be copied onto it (Topside 1.6.112 does that by itself) - no reset button needed.
- Done in the build: build_fw.sh patches the kit's core before compiling (every build from now on gets it). The sketch itself only has the version bump and this note. Checked in the cavec .elf: TinyUSB_Port_EnterDFU branches to enterUf2Dfu, which writes 0x57 to GPREGRET and resets.
- Everything else as 1.17.12 (the version pulled from the channel on 20 Nov). 1.17.13 = 1.17.12 + this.

Topside 1.6.112 (with it)
- Installs through the update drive whenever update mode shows one (the .uf2 copied onto XIAO-SENSE, as dragging it by hand does).
- A serial install that stops is finished through the drive when there is one.
- adafruit-nrfutil's failures are caught as they happen (it exits 0 when it fails).
- "Port in use" (Zach's 22 Nov "Access is denied" on COM4) and "update mode stopped answering" have their own messages; the old "safe in update mode: unplug it, plug it back in" is gone.

The test for Sully (bench unit, USB)
1. Topside 1.6.112: update the unit to 1.17.13. This first update still goes the old serial way (the unit's current firmware opens serial-only update mode). Expect it to finish as before.
2. Then Updates > Rescue > Force update mode (or install 1.17.13 again). Expect the XIAO-SENSE drive to appear in Explorer within a few seconds, and the update log to say "Update drive ... copying the firmware there as a .uf2".
3. Optional, the real proof: in update mode with the drive showing, start an install and pull the USB cable at about half way, plug it back in. The drive should come back (the unit has no firmware, so the bootloader stays with its drive); Install again finishes it.

Sim: not run - the sketch's code is unchanged (version strings only); the change is in the USB core, which the simulator doesn't model.

Tacheo firmware 1.17.11 - "OPEN" command: the phone opens Cross section on the unit
=====================================================================================
Built 19 Nov 2026 01:28 PT for cavec (T1 / v1 cave unit with the colour screen), cave (T1 OLED) and sap5xc.
Not for Drive until Sully has tried it on a unit.

WHAT AND WHY
Sully (19 Nov 2026): "When you tap 'cross section' button, the selected Tacheo will automatically switch the the
cross section function in the surveyors hands. Think Command and Control - the app can control the device. No words
have to be spoken, when the surveyor sees the cross section function open, they know to immediately take a cross
section there."

HOW
New console / Bluetooth commands (the phone app's Tacheo page sends them; USB works too):
  OPEN=xs      opens Tools > Cross section. At READY or a result: at once, with the message chime (so the surveyor
               looks down), then the aim page. Inside a menu or another tool (Sketch, Lineplot, Stations...): the unit
               backs out first, as a long MENU press would, then opens it. Already open: OK, nothing to do.
               Reply "OK OPEN xs". Refused "OPEN BUSY" during a shot or calibration hold, a running sweep, a countdown
               or standby - the phone shows that and the surveyor is asked by voice as before.
  OPEN=ready   leaves any tool or menu for READY. Reply "OK OPEN ready".
  OPEN?        "OPEN ready" / "OPEN result" / "OPEN xs" / "OPEN xs sweeping" / "OPEN page" / "OPEN busy" / "OPEN standby".
  anything else: "OPEN BAD (xs or ready)".
Serial line "open,xs,phone" and a usage-log mark (T 7) when the tool is opened this way.
Code: btWantXs / btWantReady flags set in handleCommand; checked in loop() at READY/RESULT (opens xsTool), in the
two-button menu state (closeMenu), and in waitButton / waitButtonReady (return -1 with leaveMenu, as a long MENU).
xsToolOpen flag set/cleared by xsTool() for OPEN? and the already-open case. Everything else as 1.17.10.

THE TEST FOR SULLY
1. Update a unit in Topside (Update a unit > the 1.17.11 package for its build).
2. Phone app > Tacheo page > pick that unit > tap "Cross section". The unit should chime (two notes) and show the
   Cross section aim page ("Point it along the passage, then start"). Take one as usual.
3. Open the unit's menu, tap "Cross section" on the phone again: the menu closes and the aim page opens.
4. Tap "Back to Ready" on the phone (OPEN=ready): the unit returns to READY.
5. Start a shot hold and tap "Cross section" during it: the phone should say the unit is busy, nothing changes on the unit.

SIM RESULTS (cloud container, Firmware sim of 19 Nov, all 243 scenarios, new sketch vs the 1.17.10 trunk sketch)
  cavec : 2246 pass / 54 fail  - identical to 1.17.10 except v11711_open 13/13 PASS (new)
  cave  : 1745 pass / 55 fail  - identical to 1.17.10 except v11711_open 16/16 PASS (new; the two-button menu case)
  sap5xc: 2292 pass / 42 fail  - identical to 1.17.10 except v11711_open 13/13 PASS (new)
  The 54/55/42 fails and 3 segfaults (corrupt_copy, migrate_v1, v1103_start_times) are the same lines on the 1.17.10
  baseline in this container (simfs / renames on this filesystem) - not from this change.
  Flash: cavec 98% (796068 of 811008 bytes), sap5xc 96%, cave 79%. cavec has ~15 kB left - the next feature on
  cavec needs trimming somewhere.

Tacheo firmware 1.16.98 (Tacheo 1 colour screen = cavec, Tacheo 1 OLED = cave, SAP5 with the colour screen = sap5xc)
- READY: the laser dot is always lit (Sully, 9 Nov 2026: "When on ready, always have the laser on. Whenever a user wants the laser off,
  he can take a shot and keep it on the data screen."). No minute's time-out on READY, no laser switch-off with it, and the dot is lit
  as soon as READY shows after switching on. It still goes out on the data screen (a shot's result), in the menus and in standby.
- Standby: Nordic's fix for the FPU interrupt (nRF52840 errata 87, DevZone 12433). Ordinary float maths sets FPSCR's flags, which pend
  the FPU interrupt (IRQ 38), and a pending interrupt stops the processor sleeping (TCH-260004 on 1.16.96: 89.7 % awake in standby,
  IRQ 38 pending). The FPU interrupt is now enabled at the lowest priority and its handler clears the FPSCR saved on the stack, which
  FreeRTOS restores - clearing it before each sleep alone (1.16.97) can be undone by a task switch.
- The QSPI interrupt (IRQ 41) is cleared when the logs' flash is closed and while parked (DevZone 55476).
- DIAG SLEEPIRQ: FPU interrupts handled, FPU / QSPI pendings cleared in standby, the interrupts seen pending in two standby looks in a
  row (stuck), all seen, and those pending now.
- cavec keeps 1.16.97's loggers (STBYLOG, OFFCRUMB, OFFPINS2, Device > Fully-off test) and quiet park. cave and sap5xc get the shared
  code since their last versions (cave 1.16.95, sap5xc 1.16.93: 1.16.95's laser kept powered for compass reads).
Test: READY left 2 minutes - the dot stays lit; a shot - the data screen, dot off; a press - READY, dot lit at once. Standby 1-2 days
unplugged, then a Topside report: STBYLOG awake % a few % (cavec), SLEEPIRQ stuck=00000000/00000000.

# 4 Nov 2026: SAP5 with a XIAO and the colour screen (sap5xc). Everything in 1.16.92, plus (Sully: "fix all issues", after Paul's reports):
- FIXED: a calibration that "failed to save" (Paul's colour T1, TCH-A70CD4C0: a Normal calibration finished at 0.17 and was gone after the next restart; the same unit had lost its serial number on 28 Oct). The unit's internal storage had stopped taking writes, so the new calibration lived in memory only. Now a save that fails repairs the storage on the spot - wipes it and writes back everything in memory (settings with the new calibration, serial number, owner, Options, QC, screen settings, history, backsight pairs) - and saves again. Once a power-on.
- NEW: a storage check at every switch-on from cold: a small write read back. Storage that takes no writes is repaired before anything else is saved to it.
- What a repair loses: the saved calibration holds and calibration records from before (the calibration itself is kept). The 2 MB chip (logs, animations, fonts) isn't touched. The trip log gets an F record; Topside 1.6.55 shows it ("The internal storage repaired itself"). DIAG adds FSHEAL repairs=N.
- SERIAL= / SERIAL!= from Topside repairs the storage and tries again if its write fails.
- AFTER INSTALLING on a unit that showed "Axes are not set" or lost its calibration: Factory > Axes (level, laser to north; then laser straight up), then Normal > All sensors outdoors.
- Built 4 Nov 2026. Simulator: the full scenario list on the sap5xc build, the same results as 1.16.92. The repair itself runs only on the hardware (the simulator has no flash). Not tried on a unit yet.
- Install: plugged in and running, Topside offers it (Updates and Upgrades > Tacheo). Or copy Tacheo_v1.16.93_sap5xc.uf2 onto the XIAO's drive (reset button twice quickly).
